Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Minesweeper Classic does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • The app has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • The app does not connect to the internet.
  • The app contains no ads, analytics, or tracking.
  • Your best times and your last chosen difficulty are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
