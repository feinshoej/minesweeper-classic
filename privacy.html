Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Minesweeper: Retro Edition does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • The app has no accounts of its own and contains no ads, analytics, or tracking.
  • Your best times, your last chosen difficulty, your statistics and your sound and haptics settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • If you choose to sign in to Apple's Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Game Center, which Apple runs under Apple's privacy policy. We don't receive any personal information from it.

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
