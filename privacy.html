Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Minesweeper: Retro Edition does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • The app has no accounts of its own and contains no ads, analytics, or tracking.
  • Your best times, your last chosen difficulty, your game in progress, your statistics, your Daily Board results and streak, and your settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • The optional daily reminder is a local notification that your iPhone schedules and shows by itself. Nothing about it is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • If you choose to sign in to Apple's Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Game Center, which Apple runs under Apple's privacy policy. We don't receive any personal information from it.

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
